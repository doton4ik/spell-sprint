### your-vs-youre — Your vs you're
Rule: Your shows that something belongs to you. You're is short for you are.
Memory hint: If you can say "you are", use you're.

### a-or-the — A or the: new or already known?  (article rule)
Rule: Use a / an when you mention something for the first time or mean any one of its kind. Use the when the listener already knows which one: it was mentioned before, it is clear from the situation, or there is only one.
Memory hint: A: any one, new. The: that one, we both know it.
Existing examples (do not repeat; 9):
