I attached full-library.csv — the complete word library (973 rows) of "Spell Sprint", a spelling and
vocabulary trainer for Russian-speaking learners of English, levels A2–C1.

How the app uses each row: the learner sees the Russian "translation" and types the English "word";
in other modes the learner hears the word (dictation), translates it into Russian, or chooses the
correct spelling among misspellings. After answering, the learner sees "example" and "definition".
"spelling_hint" is shown after a mistake (it is empty for most words). "alternatives" are other
English answers accepted as correct. Column "set": "base library" is studied by everyone; the
"... pack" sets are optional add-on packs for people who need that field.

Please do a DEEP QUALITY AUDIT of the whole file. Check every row. Use learner dictionaries,
English Vocabulary Profile / Oxford 3000-5000 and corpora as evidence. Do not copy copyrighted
dictionary sentences; when you propose a new example, write an original sentence.

Find and report these problems:

1. WRONG OR MISLEADING TRANSLATIONS
   The Russian translation does not match the English word, is a false friend, is too narrow,
   or belongs to another English word (example of a real past bug: sore = "болезненный",
   but "болезненный" is painful; dizzy = "головокружительный" is wrong).
2. AMBIGUOUS PROMPTS
   A Russian translation for which a learner could reasonably type a different correct English
   word (e.g. "мусор" → rubbish, but trash and garbage are also right). Propose either a more
   precise Russian prompt or the extra English answers to accept.
3. WORDS THAT DO NOT BELONG
   In the BASE library: words that are too rare, too specialised, too formal, old-fashioned,
   offensive, or free word combinations that are not real lexical units ("take out the rubbish",
   "window seat" were removed for this reason). Also words whose level clearly does not match
   "difficulty".
4. DUPLICATES
   The same word (same meaning) in several rows, or two rows whose translations are so close that
   the learner cannot know which English word is expected.
5. WEAK EXAMPLES AND DEFINITIONS
   Examples that are unnatural, too short, do not show the meaning, use the word in another
   meaning, use American spelling (the app teaches British English), or are about logistics in the
   base library. Definitions that use harder words than the headword or are wrong.
6. MISSING ESSENTIAL WORDS
   Frequent A2–B2 words (Oxford 3000 / EVP) that are hard to SPELL for Russian speakers and are
   missing from the base library. Up to 60, grouped by topic, each with a Russian translation.
7. SPELLING HINTS
   For the 100 rows where a hint would help most (the most commonly misspelled words), propose a
   short spelling_hint (max 60 characters), e.g. "double c, double m", "silent b", "-ence not -ance".

OUTPUT

are affected in each category.
